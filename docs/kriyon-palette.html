Kriyon · Design System · Color

The Kriyon palette

The BisBig direction: one flat cool blue-black plane, hairline borders, a long cool ink ramp, and a single configurable accent that punctuates. Regions are told apart by a one-step lightness shift and a 1px line — never by a gradient, a blur, or an elevation; outside a true overlay nothing casts a shadow. Every value below is a live token; nothing in the app hard-codes color.

03

Text ramp

A six-stop cool ink ramp. On the #0d1017 plane only Muted and brighter clear AA, so the ramp is a rule as much as a scale: Muted and up for anything the user reads, Dim for icons and gutters (it clears the 3:1 a non-text indicator owes, and nothing more), Faint for decoration alone.
