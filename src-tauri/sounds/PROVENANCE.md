# Notification sound provenance

All notification sounds in this directory are **original works synthesized for
Kriyon** — no recording, sample, or asset from any third party was used. Each is
generated by a small pure-Python modal-synthesis script (sums of decaying modal
partials excited by a mallet-strike transient), tuned from the *measured acoustic
partial ratios* of real instruments. They are owned by the project and carry **no
third-party license** — free to bundle and redistribute in the signed app.

Format: mono 16-bit PCM WAV, 44.1 kHz (valid for `UNNotificationSound`; ≤30s).

| bell.wav | Struck-glass bell, minor-third series 0.5 : 1 : 1.2 : 1.5 : 2 | Synthesized (Kriyon) | Project-owned, no third-party license |

If any sound is ever replaced by externally sourced audio, it MUST be verified
CC0 / public-domain, and its source URL + license recorded in this table before
it is committed.
